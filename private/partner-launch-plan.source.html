Day 1

Set up your account

  1. Open your welcome link from your welcome email and choose a password (8+ characters). You're signed in right away. The link works once; if it expires, ask us for a new one.
  2. Bookmark reellistingstudio.com. From now on, sign in there with your email and password. Forgot it? Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.
  3. Open Agency tools → Your details. Check your name, phone and email. They sign every script automatically.
  4. Open Agency tools → My link & leads. You'll see your personal link. Want something easy to say out loud? Type a custom link (letters, numbers, dashes) and press Save my link. Your old link keeps working and keeps crediting you.
  5. Open your link yourself to see what agents see: your name in the banner, your contact card next to the request form.
Tip: pick a link that's short and easy to spell over the phone, like /listingvideos. Avoid anything that looks like a brokerage name.

Days 1–2

Make your first video

  1. Add the listing. Paste a Zillow, Airbnb, VRBO or MLS link and press Import listing, or drag in the photos. If a link won't import, use the free Chrome Grabber (the link under the import box).
  2. Pick the photos. They appear as thumbnails. Tap × to remove one, drag to reorder. Photo 1 opens the video. 8–12 strong photos work best.
  3. Choose your video. Where it's going (Social, YouTube, MLS, or all three), a style, and AI motion: Every shot is recommended. Add your name, phone and logo for branded versions.
  4. Press Make my video. Keep the tab open; it takes a few minutes. Each shot is checked automatically, so fans spin and nothing looks frozen.
  5. Check the shot report under the video. If a shot says it barely moves, press Re-make.
  6. Save your video and watch it on your phone. That's your demo.

Getting the best result

  • Professional listing photos look best. Rooms with ceiling fans, fireplaces, pools and hot tubs come alive the most.
  • Each AI shot uses 1 credit; a video with every shot animated uses up to about 12, including automatic re-makes.

Answers to the questions you'll hear

"Is it fake?"
No. It animates their real listing photos. Nothing is added or removed. Suggest the caption line "Motion enhanced from listing photos."
"Will the MLS take it?"
Every package includes an unbranded MLS version. Rules vary, so they should check their MLS.
"Does it look like a slideshow?"
No. Show your demo: fans spin, flames flicker, the camera moves through each room.
"Why so cheap vs. a videographer?"
No shoot, no travel, no second staging. It's built from photos they already paid for.
"My photos aren't great."
They send them anyway. If they won't make a great video, we say so before they pay anything.
"How fast?"
Within 24 hours on every package. 12-hour rush for $75.

Days 3–7

Share your link

  1. Make a list of 25 agents you know: past clients, colleagues, people in your network. Warm contacts first.
  2. Send the intro text. Agency tools → Scripts → Intro to an agent you know → Copy. It already has your name, number and link. Just swap in their first name.
  3. Post a video on your own social accounts with your link. Agency tools → Demo & ad videos has the commercial and examples, ready to download. Add "I partner with Reel Listing Studio" or #ad to the post.
  4. Watch for new listings from agents in your area and send the New listing congrats text or DM the same day.
  5. Follow up 3–5 days later with the follow-up script if they haven't replied.
Hi [Agent], it's [you]. Quick one: I'm now making listing walkthrough videos straight from your listing photos, no shoot needed. Ceiling fans spin, fireplaces flicker and pools ripple, so it looks filmed. Here's a short example: [your link]
Want me to make one for your next listing?

When an agent says yes

Handle every lead fast

  1. You'll see the request in Agency tools → My link & leads with the agent's name, phone, email, listing and package, and by email once alerts are switched on.
  2. Reach out the same day, ideally within the hour. Thank them, confirm the listing and the package, and answer questions.
  3. Reel Listing Studio takes it from there: reviews the photos, sends the invoice for approval and delivers the video within 24 hours.
  4. Check in after delivery and ask for their next listing. Happy agents order again and refer their office.

Agents who request a video or create an account through your link within 90 days are credited to you automatically. They don't need to mention your name.

Partner rules

  • Be upfront about the partnership. When you post or recommend Reel Listing Studio, say you're a partner (for example "#ad" or "I partner with Reel Listing Studio").
  • Text people who know you or asked to hear from you, and stop right away if someone says STOP or asks you to.
  • Describe it honestly. It animates real photos; don't promise results, sale prices or anything not listed in the packages.
  • No brokerage names or logos, yours or anyone else's, in your posts about Reel Listing Studio without that brokerage's written permission.
  • Keep agents' information private. Use it only to follow up on their request.
