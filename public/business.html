Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need a photographer?

No. Clear phone photos work. Shoot in good light, hold the phone steady and fill the frame with the thing you sell. We pick the best and touch them up.

Will it change my food or my work?

No. Only what's already in the photo moves. Nothing is added, nothing disappears, and your signs and logo stay readable. If a shot isn't right, we re-make it.

Can I run it as a paid ad?

Yes. You get the sizes Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube ask for, and the music is an original score made for each video, so there's nothing to license.

What do I send?

4 to 6 photos, your offer (like "Free first class" or "Order tonight"), your phone or booking link, and your logo if you have one.

Can I make them myself?

Yes. Members get the studio: add your photos, type your name and offer, and it builds the reel for you. Ask your rep about a monthly plan.
