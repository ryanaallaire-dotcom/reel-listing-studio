FAQ

Fair questions. Straight answers.

Is this fake or misleading to buyers?

No. Every frame comes from your actual listing photos. We add motion (a fan turning, a fire flickering, the camera moving through the room), but nothing is added to the property or removed from it. No virtual furniture, no erased power lines. See the before and after clips. For full transparency, add “Motion enhanced from listing photos” to the caption. We’ll include that line if you ask.

Will it look like a slideshow?

No. A slideshow pans across still pictures. This looks like someone walked through the home with a camera: things in each room move, and the camera moves through the space. Watch the before and after clips and judge for yourself.

Can I put it on the MLS?

Yes. Every package includes an unbranded, MLS-ready version with no names, logos or contact info. MLS rules vary, so check yours.

What if my photos aren’t great?

We look before you pay. If your photos won’t make a great video, we tell you up front and you owe nothing. Most professional listing photos work well.

How fast is it, really?

24 hours for every package, and the Full Launch goes to the top of the queue. Need it sooner? Add 12-hour rush for $75.

What do I actually have to do?

Send the listing link or a photo folder. That’s it. No scheduling, no seller coordination, no second cleanup.

What if I want changes?

The Social Pack includes 1 revision and the Full Launch includes 2. On the Listing Reel, we fix anything that’s our mistake, like a frozen shot or a wrong detail, at no charge.

Who owns the video? Can I run ads with it?

You can post it anywhere, boost it, run it as an ad and show it in listing presentations. The Social Pack and Full Launch include ad copy, and with the Full Launch we build and launch the ads for you.

Why does it cost less than a videographer?

Nobody drives to the property, nobody books a shoot, and the home isn’t staged twice. We work from the photos you already paid for.

Is it worth it on a mid-priced listing, or a rental?

Yes. At $199 you get all three versions and a caption, when published rates run $350 for one 30-second vertical alone, it makes a mid-priced listing look like a luxury one, and it’s a strong piece for your next listing presentation. Rentals get a dedicated Airbnb or VRBO version for $99.
